Work with us

Join the lab

We welcome curious students who want to do real research and ship real results.

Why join

  1. Learn and work on interesting topics — no matter what we achieve together, you will learn something new and meaningful.
  2. Deliver results — we have a goal-oriented mindset that leads to real personal and professional accomplishments.
  3. Connect and thrive — we work professionally, in a relaxed environment.

What's expected

Minimum weekly commitment:

  • Research project: 15 hours full-time, or 5 hours part-time
  • Professional development: 2–3 hours
  • Weekly lab meetings: 1–2 hours
  • Other activities: 1 hour

How to join

  1. Fill out the member form — this step is required.
  2. Once accepted, enroll in a research course; you will get instructions by email.
  3. Get assigned to a team and project based on your interests.
